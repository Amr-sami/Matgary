At launch (0–50 active tenants) you can run everything on a single Hostinger KVM 2 VPS with weekly backup snapshots for about $19/month fixed. Variable costs are dominated by Gemini API (~$0.90/tenant/mo at typical AI usage) and SMS OTP (~$0.02/tenant/mo using a local Egyptian SMS provider).

Pricing EGP 399/mo (one flat tier) with discounts for 3-month and annual prepay gives a gross margin above 85% once you clear ~15 paying tenants. The “first month free” promotion costs you at most $1/signup in variable cost — cheaper than paid acquisition would be.

First VPS upgrade trigger: ~50 active tenants (move KVM 2 → KVM 4, +$3/mo). Second upgrade: ~200 tenants (KVM 4 → KVM 8, +$8/mo). You stay below $50/month total infra cost until you cross 500 active tenants.

Scaling math (rough): One KVM 2 (2 vCPU / 8 GB) comfortably serves ~50 active shops each with 2–5 concurrent users during peak sales hours. One KVM 4 serves ~200. One KVM 8 serves ~500. Above 500 the bottleneck is Postgres — move it to Hostinger Managed DB or Neon/Supabase ($20–40/mo) and keep the app on your VPS.

6 · Launch strategy & marketing budget

Egyptian SMB shop owners live on Facebook groups, WhatsApp, and TikTok — not LinkedIn. Spend accordingly.

6.1   The “first month free” offer, costed honestly

A trial tenant costs you ≈ $0.06 in variable costs over 30 days. Even if only 20% of trials convert to paid, your acquisition cost per paying customer via the free-trial route is roughly EGP 15 (the 5 trials × EGP 3). Compare that to ~EGP 50–100 CPA via Facebook Ads for the same audience — the free trial IS your acquisition channel. Make it easy: no credit card, instant access, one-tap-to-upgrade inside the app.

6.2   Phase-by-phase marketing budget (6 months)

Phase Channel Monthly spend Target CPA Target signups
Month 1–2 — pre-launch beta Direct outreach, Facebook group posts in 3–5 shop-owner groups, WhatsApp referrals EGP 0 (time only) EGP 0 20–40 beta users
Month 3 — public launch FB/IG ads (Arabic creative), 1 TikTok shopkeeper collab, local YouTube tutorial EGP 3,000 EGP 50 60 signups
Month 4–5 — scale FB/IG ads (double down on best-performing creative), WhatsApp Business broadcast, 2–3 TikTok collabs EGP 5,000 EGP 40 125 signups / mo
Month 6+ — retention & referrals Referral programme (1 month free per successful referral), case-study posts from happy tenants EGP 3,000 (ads) + EGP 2,000 (referral rewards) EGP 25 200+ signups / mo

6.3   Specific campaign tactics worth trying

  • Launch WhatsApp group “أصحاب المحلات مع Storo” — 100% free, builds trust faster than any paid ad. Pin a short 60-second demo video.
  • TikTok shopkeeper POV — pay one popular shop-owner creator EGP 2–5k to post “my day with Storo.” Egyptian SMB TikTok reaches hundreds of thousands at that price.
  • Arabic YouTube tutorial series (5 videos × 4 min each) — “افتح متجرك في 10 دقايق،” “قدم استيراد من إكسل.” Zero ongoing cost, compounds forever.
  • Referral: 1 month free per paying referral, capped at 6 months/account. You give up EGP 399 but gain a tenant with ~50% lower churn (referred users are stickier).
  • Hardware reseller partnerships — 20% revenue share with POS printer / barcode-scanner shops that bundle Storo in their package deal. They do your field sales for free.
