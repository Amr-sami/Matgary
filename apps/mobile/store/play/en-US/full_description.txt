TheStoro is the point-of-sale system that runs your shop from your phone — supermarket, clothing store, pharmacy, café or wholesale stockroom — and stays in sync with the web dashboard at thestoro.com in real time.

REGISTER
• Scan barcodes with the phone camera; items drop straight into the cart.
• Line and receipt discounts, multiple payment methods.
• Print receipts on a Bluetooth thermal printer, or share them as an image/PDF over WhatsApp.
• Works offline: sales are recorded locally and upload themselves the moment the connection returns.

INVENTORY
• Live stock per product and per branch, with low-stock alerts.
• Add a product with barcode, photo and price in seconds.
• Quick counts, branch transfers, and returns that adjust stock automatically.

PURCHASES & SUPPLIERS
• Record purchase invoices against a supplier and track what you owe.

CUSTOMERS
• A profile per customer with phone number, purchase history and balance.
• Message customers on WhatsApp straight from their profile.

EXPENSES & REPORTS
• Log the day's expenses; see net sales, profit and best-sellers at a glance.
• A daily summary arrives as a push notification.

TEAM
• Staff accounts with per-person permissions.
• Check-in/check-out from within the shop's radius, leave requests, daily tasks.

SECURITY
• Lock the app with Face ID / Touch ID.
• Your data is encrypted and synced with your web account — a lost phone is not lost data.

TheStoro is Arabic-first by design: true right-to-left layout, Egyptian pound formatting, and every screen in your language. Also available in English.

A TheStoro account is required. Open the app and register your shop in a minute, or sign in with your existing web account.
